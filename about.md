# Globed Group Chats

Group chats for Geometry Dash, sent through [Globed](mod:dankmeme.globed2)'s servers, with emojis and level sharing.

## How to use
- Open **Group Chats** from the green chat button at the bottom of the main menu and create a group.
- Open someone's GD profile and tap the chat button on the left to add them to one of your groups.
- They get an invite (or join automatically if they've enabled *Auto-accept Invites*).
- **Emojis:** tap the smiley next to the message box, or type shortcodes like `:fire:` or `:skull:`.
- **Levels:** on any online level's page, tap the chat button on the left to send it to a group. Members can tap **View** to open it.

## Good to know
- You need to be connected to Globed. Messages only reach members who are **online and in the same Globed room as you** (usually the global room).
- Nothing is stored on a server. Each player keeps their own history, so messages sent while someone was offline won't reach them.
- Groups can have up to 16 members and messages up to 200 characters. Only the owner can add or remove members.
